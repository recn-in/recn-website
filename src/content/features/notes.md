What you want to remember about a take is usually not in the take. It is the tuning, the pedal order, the line you almost sang. So a note in RECN sits next to the audio rather than in another app.

A note is made of blocks. Text, headings, code, callouts, bulleted and numbered lists, to-dos, quotes, dividers. Type `/` to insert one. A block can also be a recording, a session, a drawing or another note, playable and openable where it sits.

## Pictures

Paste an image into a note on a Mac or PC. On a phone or iPad, take one with the camera or pick it from your library. Drag the handle to resize it on the desktop; on the phone, tap through the sizes.

## Moving a block

Press a block and lift it. A line shows where it will land, the page scrolls while you hold it, and letting go commits the move. Same gesture on a trackpad and on glass.

## Attached, or on their own

A note can belong to one or more recordings, or stand on its own in the library or inside a project.

## The same note on two devices

Note bodies merge character by character. Write a paragraph on the phone while someone rewrites an earlier one on the Mac, and when the devices meet both sets of words are there. Nothing asks you to pick a copy.
