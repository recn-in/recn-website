## Dropping one

Play until you hear it and press the marker button, or the letter M on a Mac or PC. On a phone or iPad you can also press and hold anywhere on the waveform to put a marker exactly there. A card opens for the comment and one of six colours: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple. Cancel it and nothing is left behind.

## While you listen

A marker is a slim vertical tick in its colour above the waveform. Click or tap one to read it or edit it. When playback crosses a marker, its comment appears over the waveform for about two seconds and then gets out of the way. On a phone or iPad you can hold a marker and nudge it until it sits on the right beat.

## They travel with the recording

Markers belong to the recording, not to the device that made them. Move the recording into a project and they move with it. Open it on another paired device and they are there.
