## Projects inside projects

A project holds recordings, notes, sessions, drawings and other projects, up to three levels deep. Enter one and the library shows only what is in it, in the same order and with the same rows as everywhere else.

## Knowing where you are

A bar under the header says where you stand. Click an earlier name to go back to it. Click a separator and it lists the other projects at that level, so you can step sideways without climbing out first. Click the empty part of the bar to type the name of the project you want. On a phone it is one level per screen, and the back button carries the name of the project above.

## Moving things in and out

Drag an item onto a project, or choose Move to project from its menu, which works on a whole selection at once. Move a project and everything filed inside it comes along.

## The same shape on every device

Projects sync like anything else. Make one on the Mac, file a take into it from the phone, and both devices agree on where that take now lives.
