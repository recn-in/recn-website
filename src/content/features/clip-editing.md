An arrangement is rarely right on the first pass. The second verse starts a beat late, and the count-in is sitting on the front of the take.

Drag a clip along its lane to change when it happens, or onto another lane to move it to that track. Drag an edge to trim it. Nothing you trim away is lost: pull the edge back out and the audio returns.

## What you can do to a clip

- Split at the playhead, or where you click.
- Mute one clip without muting the track.
- Duplicate, delete, or nudge by one grid step.
- Set clip gain in decibels, apart from the track level.
- Undo any of it.

## Selecting and snapping

On a computer, drag across empty space and every clip the rectangle touches is selected, across lanes. The selection then moves, copies and deletes as one. A magnet sets how coarsely things land, from the grid line down to a fraction of a beat; Option ignores it for one gesture.

On a phone, tap a clip for its actions, or Select clips from the session menu for several at once. Clips settle on bars when you are zoomed out, beats when you are zoomed in.

## Where two clips overlap

Slide a clip over the tail of another on the same track and the seam becomes an equal-power crossfade the length of the overlap. You never draw it; what is drawn is what plays. A fade can also be pulled in by hand from the clip's corner.
