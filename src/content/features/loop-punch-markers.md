## Loop a section

Lay a region over the ruler and arm it. Play starts at the loop and stop comes back to it, and the region wraps without a gap for as long as you leave it running. Drag the band to move the region, or either end to resize it. Record with the loop armed and the pass keeps wrapping too, one take per lap.

## Punch in

A punch range is set by its numbers rather than by eye, with up to eight bars of lead-in before it. Play through the lead-in the way you would play through a count-in, and only what falls inside the range is kept.

## Markers

Drop a marker at the playhead and give it a name. Drag a flag to move it. From a flag you can rename it, delete it, or loop the section from that marker to the next one, which is usually what you made the marker for.

All of it is there on a phone or tablet too, and all of it travels with the session to your other devices.
