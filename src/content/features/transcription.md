You know someone said the thing. You do not know which of forty voice memos it is in. A transcript turns that hour of listening into a search.

RECN transcribes on the device. Download a speech model once, and from then on the audio is read where it already is. No upload, no account, no queue, and it works on a plane.

## Where it runs

Transcription runs on iPhone and iPad. You pick the model: a small one that gets through a long interview quickly, or a larger one that is slower and more accurate. Each reads 99 languages and works out which one it is hearing. You can have it translate into English as it goes, or turn on a live transcript that fills in while you are still recording.

## Where it shows up

The transcript is part of the recording, so it syncs with it. On a Mac or PC the player gains a Transcript tab next to the waveform: the word at the playhead is picked out as it plays, and clicking a line jumps there. Desktop displays transcripts, it does not make them.

## Nothing leaves the device

The model file is downloaded once, from then on the work is local, and the text only ever travels to your own paired devices.
